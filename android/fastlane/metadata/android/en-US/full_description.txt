Representing yourself in a custody, divorce, protective-order, housing or small-claims case? Arcalume turns the evidence on your phone into an organized, numbered exhibit packet you can print, file or hand to a lawyer, without uploading anything.

BUILD YOUR CASE FILE
• Add photos, videos, screenshots, PDFs, Word files and voice recordings, or share them straight from WhatsApp, Gallery, Files or your mail app.
• WhatsApp chat exports and SMS Backup & Restore files become a clean transcript: every message in time order, with the date, time and sender.
• Number, describe and date each exhibit, or put them all in date order with one tap.

SEE WHAT YOUR FILES SAY ABOUT THEMSELVES
• Reads hidden metadata: when and on what phone a photo was taken, its GPS location, who created and last edited a Word file, when a PDF was made and how many times it was saved.
• Flags what the other side may question before they do: photos saved again by editing software, PDFs changed after they were first made, tracked changes and deleted text still inside a Word file, and files renamed to the wrong type.
• Finds embedded preview images and pictures hidden inside other files.

A PACKET THAT LOOKS LIKE IT BELONGS IN COURT (PRO)
• One PDF with a cover page, an index of exhibits, a chronology, a tabbed section for every exhibit, page numbers and a page stamp on every page.
• Each exhibit shows its SHA-256 fingerprint, a code computed from the file's exact bytes, so anyone can confirm it was not changed.
• Ends with a fill-in declaration for authenticating your exhibits.
• The verification bundle is a ZIP of the untouched originals, their fingerprints, the case summary and the custody log.

A CUSTODY LOG YOU CAN CHECK
• Adding, numbering, removing and exporting exhibits is recorded in a log where every entry is chained to the one before it. Editing, reordering or deleting entries breaks the chain, and the app checks it for you.

RECOVER DAMAGED DOCUMENT PHOTOS
• Evens out glare, shadow and poor light so faded text in a photo of a page comes back. Every pixel the photo held no data for is marked, and nothing is invented.
• Seals the original and the recovered copy into an encrypted vault (Argon2id and AES-256-GCM).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Arcalume does not request internet access. No account, no ads, no tracking, no cloud.
• Evidence is excluded from cloud backups. Nothing leaves the phone unless you export it.

FREE AND PRO
Free: your case file, every metadata check, chat transcripts, packets of up to three exhibits, document recovery, and opening and checking any vault.
Pro: packets with every exhibit, verification bundles, vault sealing and clean saved copies. One payment, no subscription.

WHAT IT IS NOT
Arcalume is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. It reports what your files say about themselves; it cannot prove what happened to a file before it reached your phone, and it does not certify that evidence is admissible. The court decides that.
